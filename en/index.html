Whose fault is expensive gasoline?

Dilma, Temer, Bolsonaro and Lula: what each government did with the price of gasoline and diesel, what a litre cost in each period, how much that weighs on wages and what no president controls. With the same standard for everyone.

📊 See the summary in icons

The short answer

No single person is to blame. What moves the price most comes from abroad: oil and the dollar. What each government decides is who pays the bill: Dilma held prices down at a loss to Petrobras, and Bolsonaro and Lula cut taxes in election years. Adjusted for inflation, gasoline was most expensive under Bolsonaro (an average of R$6.91 in today's money), then under Lula (R$6.52), Temer (R$6.26) and Dilma (R$5.92). Bolsonaro sold BR Distribuidora, which took away a tool the government had to bring prices down. In Minas Gerais, the 31% ICMS made gasoline about R$0.47 more expensive than in São Paulo, until 2022. Today the ICMS is the same in every state.

Where the money for a litre goes

A litre of gasoline cost an average of R$6.54 in Brazil in the week of October 4 to 10, 2026. It splits into five pieces, and each has a different owner:

R$2.08PetrobrasWho decides: Petrobras, whose main owner is the federal government
R$1.67Distributors and gas stationsWho decides: the companies. This is an estimate
R$1.57ICMSWho decides: the 27 governors, jointly, in Confaz
R$0.98Blended ethanol30% of the litre is ethanol, at market price
R$0.24Federal taxesWho decides: the federal government. They are almost zero

In other words: the president controls directly only federal taxes, and indirectly Petrobras. Oil and the dollar come from abroad, the ICMS belongs to the states, and the gas station margin belongs to the companies.

Why was the peak in 2022? Oil soared worldwide after the invasion of Ukraine, and Petrobras followed the international price. And why does Dilma have the lowest average? Partly because Petrobras sold below the price abroad, and the company took the loss.

What about diesel?

With diesel, the order changes: the highest average is Lula's. Diesel nearly doubled at the end of the Bolsonaro government, from R$3.44 in January 2019 to R$6.36 in December 2022, and Lula started at that level. Adjusted for inflation, it fell under Lula, from R$7.48 to R$6.70 in today's money.

How many litres the minimum wage buys

Another way to compare: take each month's minimum wage and see how many litres of gasoline it bought. The more litres, the better for those who earn the minimum.

Under Lula and under Dilma, the minimum wage bought about 240 litres on average. Under Bolsonaro, 217. The worst year in the series was 2021, with 192 litres. In September 2026, with a minimum wage of R$1,621, it was about 248 litres.

The minimum wage increase is also each government's decision. So this calculation mixes two things: the price of fuel and the raise in wages.

What about the sale of BR?

When Petrobras owned the BR gas stations, the government had a way to make a price cut reach the pump faster. After the sale, it no longer does. What the numbers show:

What weighs against the sale

  • The margin of distributors and gas stations on gasoline went from R$0.74 to R$1.14 per litre between 2019 and 2025, according to Ineep, using ANP data.
  • Since the start of the 2026 war, the margin on S10 diesel has risen 68.7%, according to Ibeps.
  • The government, the president of Petrobras and the Minas Gerais gas station union say cuts arrive slowly and increases arrive fast.

What weighs in its favour, with the same rigour

  • After inflation of about 43% over the period, the gasoline margin rose about 8%, not 54%.
  • Ineep, Ibeps and the Observatório Social do Petróleo are linked to oil workers, who opposed the sale. The data are ANP's, but the reading is theirs.
  • Vibra has about 22% of the market and competes with Ipiranga and Raízen. Margins rose for all of them.

Conclusion: the sale took a tool away from the government, and the margin grew. But we found no independent study, from CADE or ANP, showing how much of this is due to the sale.

What about Minas Gerais?

Until 2022, each state charged ICMS as a percentage of the price. Minas Gerais charged 31% on gasoline, one of the highest rates in the country. São Paulo charged 25%. See what that did to the price:

Minas GeraisSão Paulo
PeriodICMS on gasolineHow much more Minas Gerais paid than São Paulo, per litre
2013 to mid-2017Minas Gerais 29%, São Paulo 25%R$ 0.17
Mid-2017 to June 2022Minas Gerais 31%, São Paulo 25%. The increase to 31% was by Governor Fernando Pimentel, of the PT. Romeu Zema, of Novo, took office in 2019 and kept the 31%R$0.47. Under Zema alone, R$0.49
July 2022 to May 202318% in both, because of the federal law that capped the ICMSR$ 0.01
June 2023 to todayA fixed amount per litre, the same in every state, set jointly by the 27 governments in Confaz. Today, R$1.57R$ 0.02

In other words: yes, the Minas Gerais ICMS made gasoline more expensive, about R$0.47 per litre more than in São Paulo. The 31% rate was created in 2017 by Pimentel and kept by Zema until the federal law forced it down, in July 2022. Since 2023, the ICMS has been the same throughout the country, and the difference disappeared: in September 2026, Minas Gerais had the second-cheapest gasoline in Brazil, R$6.32, behind only Rio Grande do Sul.

Today, a governor alone cannot lower the gasoline ICMS in their state: the amount is decided jointly. The increases of 2024, 2025 and 2026 were approved by the states in Confaz.

Minas Gerais has another advantage: the Gabriel Passos refinery, Regap, in Betim, still belongs to Petrobras. Its sale was attempted and ended in 2022. In March 2026, at the start of the war, the Minas Gerais gas station union accused Vibra, the former BR, of supplying its own-brand stations first. Vibra blamed logistics and the weather.

Is it true?

True

“Gasoline was never as expensive as under Bolsonaro.” Since 2013, yes. In May 2022 a litre cost R$7.28 on average, equivalent to R$8.74 today. His government's adjusted average was the highest of the four.

False

“Gasoline is more expensive today than ever.” In September 2026 it cost R$6.54. That is less than the R$7.28 of May 2022, even without adjusting for inflation.

True

“Under Lula, the minimum wage buys more gasoline than under Bolsonaro.” On average, 242 litres against 217. Under Dilma, it was 239.

Half true

“Diesel got more expensive under Lula.” The Lula government's average is the highest. But diesel nearly doubled at the end of the Bolsonaro government, and under Lula it fell once inflation is taken into account.

Half true

“Gasoline in Minas Gerais is expensive because of Zema.” The 31% ICMS made gasoline in Minas Gerais about R$0.47 more expensive than in São Paulo. But the one who raised the rate to 31% was Fernando Pimentel, of the PT, in 2017. Zema kept it until the federal law forced it down, in 2022. Since 2023 the ICMS has been the same in every state, and Minas Gerais has some of the cheapest gasoline in the country.

Half true

“Dilma broke Petrobras by holding down gasoline prices.” The company lost money selling below the price abroad: about R$55 billion between 2011 and 2014, by CBIE's estimate. But Petrobras's crisis in those years also had other causes, such as high debt and the embezzlement uncovered by Operation Car Wash.

Half true

“Gasoline is expensive because of the privatization of BR.” The sale took away a way for the government to make cuts reach the pump, and the gas station margin rose. But, after inflation, the margin's rise up to 2025 was small, and the biggest jump came with the 2026 war.

True

“The price rose in 2026 because of the war.” Oil went above US$100 after the attack on Iran and the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz. In the second week of March alone, diesel rose 12% in Minas Gerais.

False

“The ICMS has not gone up since 2022.” The states raised the fixed amount every year: from R$1.22 per litre of gasoline in 2023 to R$1.57 in 2026.

True

“Bolsonaro zeroed the tax on gasoline in the election year.” In June 2022, until December.

True

“Lula zeroed the tax on gasoline in the election year.” On October 9, 2026, for 30 days.

Not shown

“The cut was only to win the election.” This applies to both. The timing matters, but both governments faced a sharp rise in oil to justify the measure. Motive is intent, and intent is not proven by a date.

Half true

“Lula lowered gasoline prices when he ended the PPI.” Petrobras cut R$0.40 in May 2023. But the federal tax had returned in March, and the ICMS rose every year afterwards.

In short

  • Adjusted for inflation, gasoline was most expensive under Bolsonaro and cheapest under Dilma. The minimum wage bought more litres under Lula and Dilma, and fewer under Bolsonaro.
  • What moves the price most comes from abroad: oil and the dollar. That was the case in 2021 and 2022, under Bolsonaro, and in 2026, under Lula.
  • Each government chose who pays the bill: Petrobras, under Dilma and Lula; the driver, under Temer and early in Bolsonaro's term; the Treasury and the states, under Bolsonaro in 2022 and under Lula in 2026.
  • The sale of BR, under Bolsonaro, took a tool away from the government, and the gas station margin rose. How much of that is due to the sale has not yet been measured independently.
  • The ICMS is the governors' decision, and it has risen every year since 2023.

Sources and limits of this reading

Prices are the monthly averages from ANP's gas station survey, for regular gasoline and regular diesel (S500). The ANP monthly series used here starts in January 2013, so Dilma's first term appears only for 2013 and 2014. There was no survey in September 2020. The inflation adjustment uses IBGE's IPCA and brings everything to September 2026. Averages by government assign to Temer the months from May 2016, when Dilma was suspended. A higher or lower price under a government does not prove it was that government's decision: oil and the dollar weigh more. The estimate of Petrobras's losses under Dilma comes from a private research centre, CBIE. The readings on the gas station margin come from institutes linked to oil workers, and those of the government and Vibra are interested versions.
